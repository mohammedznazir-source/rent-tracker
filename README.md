# Rent Tracker

A small rent payment tracker that installs on an Android phone like a regular app.

**Open it:** https://mohammedznazir-source.github.io/rent-tracker/

In Chrome on your phone, open the link, then tap ⋮ → **Install app**. It opens full screen from its own icon and works offline.

Your rent details and payments are stored only on your phone, never in this repository. Use **Settings → Backup** to save a copy.
